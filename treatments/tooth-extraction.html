About the Treatment

What it involves

Extraction is recommended when a tooth is severely decayed, fractured, infected beyond repair, or causing crowding that affects orthodontic treatment. At Omax Dental, extractions are performed with careful attention to comfort and are always discussed as a last option after exploring whether the tooth can be saved.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Removes a source of pain or infection
  • Prevents infection spreading to nearby teeth
  • Clears crowding where needed for orthodontic plans
  • Quick procedure with clear aftercare guidance
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. AssessmentAn X-ray confirms the tooth's position and condition.
  2. AnaesthesiaThe area is fully numbed before any extraction begins.
  3. ExtractionThe tooth is carefully removed with minimal disturbance to surrounding tissue.
  4. Aftercare GuidanceClear instructions are given to support fast, comfortable healing.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • A tooth too decayed or fractured to restore
  • Severe infection unresponsive to other treatment
  • Crowding that requires clearing space for orthodontics
  • A stubborn baby tooth not falling out naturally
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

Mild swelling and tenderness for a couple of days is normal. Sticking to soft foods and following aftercare instructions helps the socket heal smoothly within about a week.
