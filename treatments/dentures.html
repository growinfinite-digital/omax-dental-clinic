About the Treatment

What it involves

Dentures replace multiple missing teeth with a removable appliance custom-moulded to your gums and bite. Whether partial or complete, they're built to restore chewing ability and facial support, and are fitted and adjusted over a few visits to get comfort exactly right.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Restores chewing ability and facial support
  • Non-surgical, removable solution
  • Custom-fitted for comfort and stability
  • Available as partial or complete sets
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. AssessmentYour gums, jaw and remaining teeth are assessed to determine the right type.
  2. ImpressionsDetailed impressions capture the exact shape of your mouth.
  3. Trial FittingA trial denture is checked for fit, bite and appearance.
  4. Final FittingAdjustments are made until the final denture feels secure and natural.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • Several or all teeth missing in one or both jaws
  • Not a current candidate for implants
  • Wanting a removable, budget-conscious replacement option
  • Needing restored support for facial appearance
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

A short adjustment period is normal as your mouth gets used to the new denture — minor sore spots can be refined at a quick follow-up visit.
