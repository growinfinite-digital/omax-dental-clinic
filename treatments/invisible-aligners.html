About the Treatment

What it involves

Invisible aligners are a series of custom, transparent trays that gradually shift teeth into position, swapped out every couple of weeks as your smile progresses. They're removable for eating and brushing, and far less noticeable day-to-day than traditional braces, making them a popular choice for image-conscious adults.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Virtually invisible during everyday wear
  • Removable for eating, brushing and flossing
  • Fewer in-clinic adjustment visits typically needed
  • Digital planning shows your result in advance
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. Digital Scan & PlanningImpressions or a digital scan map your teeth and plan the movement sequence.
  2. Aligner FabricationA custom series of clear aligners is fabricated for your treatment.
  3. Wearing SequenceEach aligner is worn for about two weeks before moving to the next.
  4. Progress ChecksPeriodic reviews confirm teeth are tracking to plan.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • Mild to moderate crowding or spacing
  • Wanting a discreet straightening option
  • Needing a removable option for lifestyle or work reasons
  • Committed to wearing aligners as directed daily
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

Mild pressure when switching to a new aligner in the series is normal and settles within a day or two, with no impact on daily activities.
