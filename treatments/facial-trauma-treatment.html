About the Treatment

What it involves

Facial trauma — from a fall, sports injury or accident — can affect teeth, the jawbone, or surrounding facial structures. Prompt, specialist assessment matters for both function and appearance long-term. Dr. Mahesh Pund's maxillofacial surgical training covers diagnosis and treatment of these injuries, coordinated with imaging to guide the right approach.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Specialist assessment of jaw and facial injuries
  • Imaging-guided treatment planning
  • Coordinated care for both function and appearance
  • Experienced maxillofacial surgical management
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. AssessmentThe injury is examined and imaged to determine its full extent.
  2. Treatment PlanningAn approach is planned based on the type and severity of trauma.
  3. TreatmentAppropriate surgical or restorative treatment is carried out.
  4. Follow-Up CareHealing is monitored closely with scheduled follow-up visits.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • Jaw or facial injury from an accident or fall
  • Teeth affected by a facial impact
  • Persistent pain or swelling following trauma
  • Seeking a specialist opinion after an injury
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

Recovery varies significantly with the nature of the injury and is explained in detail as part of your treatment plan, with close follow-up throughout healing.
